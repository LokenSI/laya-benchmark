Business summary of local decision-model benchmarks. A supervised TF-IDF/linear SVM reached 89.1% with labelled training data. Suggested use: local category suggestions reviewed by staff; measure time saved and corrections. Queueing grew under load. Public results do not establish production accuracy or financial savings. Four Decision 2.0 models produced 70,304 saved predictions on an RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB.
